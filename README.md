# ExifGone

ExifGone is a privacy focused Android application built with Flutter that allows users to strip EXIF metadata from their photos before sharing them. Protect your privacy by removing location data, camera settings, and other sensitive information embedded in your images.

## Core Features

- **EXIF Metadata Removal**: Instantly strip GPS coordinates, device details, and timestamps from your images to ensure your privacy.
- **Local Cache Management**: Intelligent handling of processed files. The app automatically manages its local storage to prevent clutter and save space on your device.
- **Direct Social Media Sharing**: Once an image is cleaned, share it directly to your favorite platforms (Instagram, X, WhatsApp, etc.) without leaving the app.
